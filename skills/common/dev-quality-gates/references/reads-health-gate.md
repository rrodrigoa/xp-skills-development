# READS health contract and coverage gate

## When to load
Critical operations, outcomes, load or capacity behavior change.

## Procedure
Inspect affected critical journeys and their approved health contract. Trace eligible/terminal counts, error taxonomy, availability/probe semantics, duration population and true constrained resources from source to collected evidence. Verify retries, cancellation, partial failure, idle, no data and capacity cases. Require justified non-applicability; assess both new and worsened gaps, not unrelated inherited debt. Use dev-reads health-contract and coverage-review.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Approved operation/SLI contract, coverage matrix, exact source and test/backend evidence, baseline comparison; absent required evidence is UNKNOWN.

## Guardrails
No universal five-metric quota; metric names or dashboard screenshots alone cannot pass.
