# Exceptions, waivers and expiry

## When to load
A required rule cannot be satisfied within authorized scope.

## Procedure
Request exception with unique ID, rule, precise scope, revision/time applicability, reason, risk, alternatives, compensating controls, owner, approver and expiry. Reviewer verifies independent approval and conditions at evaluated revision. Expired, mismatched or revoked exceptions are invalid.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Approved record, evidence that conditions hold, review date and remediation issue; gate shows WAIVED, not PASS.

## Guardrails
Author cannot self-waive. Nonwaivable policy remains binding. Missing approval means original rule still applies; no permanent blanket exception.
