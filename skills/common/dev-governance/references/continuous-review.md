# Continuous governance and quality trends

## When to load
Reviewing standards over time or across projects.

## Procedure
On approved cadence review introduced/resolved debt, escape defects, blocked/held gates, waiver expiry, false positives and verification coverage. Segment by risk and project; use trends to target standards or training improvements. Record changes via policy lifecycle.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Period, data sources, denominators, trends, prioritized owner actions and follow-up review.

## Guardrails
Do not rank individuals or optimize metrics at expense of correctness. No automatic surveillance or background process in this repository.

## Observability review
Review service SLO/budget, coverage/unknowns, instrumentation regressions, cardinality/cost, alert actionability and missed incidents, monitoring pipeline reliability and ownership. Use dev-alerting alert-value for portfolio measures and dev-observability telemetry-health for coverage. Version policy changes and do not hide regressions by resetting baselines.
