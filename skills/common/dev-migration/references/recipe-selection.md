# Refactoring recipe selection

## When to load
Selecting a transformation from observed design problems.

## Procedure
Describe the current symptom, affected callers, state, policy and variation. Choose one smallest refactoring or pattern, compare a simpler alternative, and identify prerequisites. For pattern-specific steps read only the matching `oo-patterns` card.

## Required evidence and acceptance
A plan states behavior to preserve, seam, ordering, rollback and tests, with justification for each new abstraction.

## Guardrails
Never choose a pattern by popularity; do not combine broad cleanups with behavioral fixes without explicit scope.
