# Calibrating quantitative gates

## When to load
Setting thresholds for coverage, complexity, performance or agent quality.

## Procedure
Collect representative baseline and known good/bad changes. Record metric definition and sample uncertainty; estimate false blocks and missed defects. Select threshold and tolerance with owners; run advisory/shadow period, then approve enforcement and review date.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Calibration evidence, rationale, known limitations, selected values, owner and monitoring plan.

## Guardrails
Suggested numbers are starting points only. Do not choose after seeing a candidate to force its pass or compare unlike environments.
