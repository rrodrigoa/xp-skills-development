# Quality baselines and debt inventory

## When to load
Adopting standards for a legacy repository.

## Procedure
Capture trusted baseline revision and measurement methods, scope, findings, severity, owner and remediation target. Separate tolerated inherited debt from approved exceptions. Compare new/worsened debt under same method, preserve fixes and periodically ratchet down debt after approval.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Versioned baseline and concrete finding fingerprints with paths/semantic identity; every accepted debt item has owner and disposition.

## Guardrails
Baseline is not blanket waiver for security or mandatory controls. Never silently refresh baseline to hide regressions.
