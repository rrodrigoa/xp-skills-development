# Incidents and escaped defect feedback

## When to load
A production failure or missed gate exposes a quality gap.

## Procedure
Collect factual timeline, impact and contributing conditions. Identify missing or ineffective controls, evidence and detection; propose targeted scenario, guidance and gate improvements. Verify fix and independently review any policy change.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Incident-to-control mapping, regression scenario, action owner and tracked closure evidence.

## Guardrails
Avoid blame and hindsight-only requirements. Do not expose sensitive logs or use incident as reason for blanket restrictions.
