# Versioned standards and policy upgrades

## When to load
Changing skills, rules, definitions or thresholds.

## Procedure
Record immutable release/version and change rationale, compatibility and migration effects. Distinguish editorial guidance from new required gates and breaking interpretation. Review proposed changes against representative scenarios, approve and set effective date. Pin each evaluation to approved policy revision.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Changelog, old/new rule mappings, approval, effective date and rollout/rollback procedure.

## Guardrails
No retroactive blocking criteria for in-flight work without approved transition. Never evaluate a PR with its own untrusted policy changes.
