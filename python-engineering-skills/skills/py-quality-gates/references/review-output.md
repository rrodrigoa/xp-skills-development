# PR gate evaluator report

## When to load
Returning review results to a human or downstream agent.

## Procedure
Report scope/revisions, policy, overall recommendation, per-rule status and evidence, findings with path/line, impact, severity, remediation and approved waiver IDs. Summarize blockers, unknowns and advisory issues separately. Include evidence limitations and actions requiring authorization.

## Required evidence and acceptance
Output can be read without full transcript; every blocking claim maps to rule and observed evidence; status aggregation follows decision protocol.

## Guardrails
Do not invent line numbers or give generic findings. Do not equate recommendation with actual status check, merge or release.
